Every Fall 2026 5×7 card is the same object: charcoal field, 6px Concourse Red rails on the top and bottom edges, and a 1.5px hairline inset 6px from every side. Inner padding is 14–22px. Export at 300 dpi (1500 × 2100) with zoom: 3.125.

Masthead is always centered: wordmark, italic tagline, Cortado title. Body then either splits into two columns (food / pints) or stays a single cocktail list.

Front — Happy Hour. Two-column grid, 20px gutter. Item name and price share a baseline. Description tucks under the name in champagne. Specialty cocktails are not listed here — they get an outlined callout (red stroke, no fill) and a muted “see reverse side” line.

Reverse — Specialty Cocktails. Single column. 12px bowling-ball bullets, odd items red, even items champagne. One shared price at the foot: dollar in Libre Baskerville hot red, giant Bebas numeral in white, italic “each” in champagne, between two 2.5px red rules.

Do

  • Name the item. List the ingredients. Stop.
  • Food descriptions in uppercase so they read as a garnish line.
  • Cocktail recipes in ordinary sentence case — they are longer and need to breathe.
  • Keep prices on the name line, never on the description line.
  • Required footer: not valid on holidays, allergen notice.

Do not

  • Add a fifth typeface, or a new red, gold, or green.
  • Fill the specialty callout. It is an outline so the title stays loudest.
  • Set descriptions in white. That fights the item names.
  • Write “delicious,” “signature,” or exclamation marks. These boards list. They do not sell.
  • Put a price on every cocktail if they share one price. The badge is the price.
