# 03 — Broadsheet

**Idea.** The warm, appetising opposite of a digital board. Newsprint editorial:
a masthead across the top, narrow justified columns, serif display with small
caps, dotted price leaders, two inks on cream paper.

**Type.** Libre Baskerville for display/body; Source Serif 4 as a sturdy
companion for section labels. Tabular lining figures for prices.

**Tone.** Handmade, food-forward, a little literary. Feels like something you'd
find on a café table — not a franchise laminate.
