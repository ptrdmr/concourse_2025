# 02 — Marquee

**Idea.** Legibility first, for a loud dim room. Bowling-alley signage energy:
item names roughly 3× today's size, descriptions cut to a short line or dropped,
categories as full-bleed colour fields in a panel grid, prices oversized.

**Type.** One heavy geometric sans — Archivo Black for names, Archivo for
supporting copy. No script, no serif.

**Tone.** Bold, high-contrast, fun without being childish. Designed to be
readable at arm's length across a lane-side table.
