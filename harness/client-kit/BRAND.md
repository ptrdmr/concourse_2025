# Northgate Social — brand notes

Plain English, for whoever is writing the words. The AI reads this before it
touches a board.

## Who we are

A neighbourhood café that turns into a cocktail bar after five. Counter service
during the day, table service at night.

- **Name in print:** Northgate Social
- **Short name, for tight spaces:** Northgate
- **Tagline:** Coffee · Kitchen · Cocktails
- **Web:** northgatesocial.example
- **Ordering:** order.northgatesocial.example
- **Phone:** (555) 014-2200
- **Instagram:** @northgatesocial

## How we sound

Warm, plainspoken, a little proud. Never salesy, never cute.

**Do**

- Name the ingredients and stop
- Say "local" only when it is true and we can name the farm
- Headings in capitals, descriptions in ordinary sentence case

**Do not**

- Exclamation marks
- The words "delicious", "amazing", "artisanal", "elevated"
- Emoji on anything that goes on a screen or on paper
- Made-up superlatives about awards we have not won

Good: `Roast mushrooms, gruyere, caramelised onion, rye`
Bad: `Our AMAZING signature mushroom melt — a customer favourite!`

## Lines we are required to show

Copy these exactly. They are not for editing.

- **Allergens:** Items may contain nuts, dairy, gluten or shellfish. Tell your
  server about any allergy.
- **Alcohol:** Please drink responsibly. We reserve the right to refuse service.
- **Happy hour:** Happy hour is bar and patio seating only, and not valid on
  holidays.

## Quirks worth knowing

- The patio is seasonal, so anything mentioning it needs a date on it.
- The Tuesday special changes weekly and is never printed — screens only.
- Prices on the food board and the bar board must not contradict each other. If
  they do, that is a mistake, not a decision.
